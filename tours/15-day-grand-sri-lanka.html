YOUR ROUTE THROUGH SRI LANKA

15-Day Grand Sri Lanka

A wide-ranging private journey across Sri Lanka’s northern heritage, eastern beaches, ancient heartland, central hills and vibrant capital.

DAY BY DAY

The journey, unfolded.

Each day can be adjusted around your pace, interests and arrival details.

Day 1Airport to Negombo

Ease into the island with the beach, the historic Dutch Canal and the lively fish market.

  • Airport pickup
  • Negombo Beach
  • Dutch Canal
  • Fish Market
  • Overnight in Negombo
Day 2Negombo to Wilpattu

Travel north for a jeep safari through Wilpattu National Park’s forest and natural lakes.

  • Transfer to Wilpattu
  • National park safari
  • Overnight near Wilpattu
Day 3Wilpattu to Mannar

Explore Mannar’s layered history and distinctive landscape, from the old fort to its famous baobab and Talaimannar.

  • Mannar Fort
  • Baobab Tree
  • Talaimannar
  • Overnight in Mannar
Day 4Mannar to Jaffna

Continue to Jaffna and discover its fort, restored public library and revered Nallur temple.

  • Jaffna Fort
  • Jaffna Public Library
  • Nallur Temple
  • Overnight in Jaffna
Day 5Jaffna

A full northern day reaching Point Pedro, Keerimalai and the calm shallows of Casuarina Beach.

  • Point Pedro
  • Keerimalai
  • Casuarina Beach
  • Overnight in Jaffna
Day 6Jaffna to Trincomalee

Cross to the east coast and finish the day with a relaxed evening at Nilaveli Beach.

  • Drive to Trincomalee
  • Nilaveli Beach
  • Overnight in Trincomalee
Day 7Trincomalee

Travel by boat to Pigeon Island for snorkelling, then return for more time by the sea.

  • Pigeon Island National Park
  • Snorkelling
  • Beach time
  • Overnight in Trincomalee
Day 8Trincomalee

Explore Trincomalee’s dramatic temple setting, colonial fort and the warm wells of Kanniya.

  • Koneswaram Temple
  • Fort Frederick
  • Kanniya Hot Springs
  • Overnight in Trincomalee
Day 9Trincomalee to Sigiriya

Return inland for a village experience and a sunset climb at Pidurangala.

  • Drive to Sigiriya
  • Village experience
  • Pidurangala sunset
  • Overnight in Sigiriya
Day 10Sigiriya

Spend the day among two of the Cultural Triangle’s defining landmarks.

  • Sigiriya Rock Fortress
  • Dambulla Cave Temple
  • Overnight in Sigiriya
Day 11Sigiriya to Kandy

Travel through Matale’s spice-growing country before an evening visit to Kandy’s sacred temple.

  • Matale Spice Garden
  • Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic
  • Overnight in Kandy
Day 12Kandy

Discover Peradeniya’s botanical collections, walk by Kandy Lake and explore the city.

  • Royal Botanical Gardens
  • Kandy Lake
  • Kandy city tour
  • Overnight in Kandy
Day 13Kandy to Colombo

Return to the capital for a city tour and an evening at Galle Face Green.

  • Drive to Colombo
  • Colombo city tour
  • Galle Face Green
  • Overnight in Colombo
Day 14Colombo

Spend a full day among Colombo’s temple, market streets and shopping districts.

  • Gangaramaya Temple
  • Pettah Market
  • Shopping
  • Overnight in Colombo
Day 15Colombo to the Airport

Keep the final hours flexible before your private airport transfer.

  • Free time in Colombo
  • Airport transfer and drop-off
