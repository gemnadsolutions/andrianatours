YOUR ROUTE THROUGH SRI LANKA

4-Day Sigiriya Tour

A compact private journey through Sri Lanka’s Cultural Triangle, combining rock fortresses, village life, ancient cities and an elephant safari.

DAY BY DAY

The journey, unfolded.

Each day can be adjusted around your pace, interests and arrival details.

Day 1Airport to Sigiriya

A personal airport welcome begins your journey. Travel inland to visit Dambulla Cave Temple before checking in and relaxing in Sigiriya.

  • Airport pickup and assistance
  • Transfer to Sigiriya
  • Visit Dambulla Cave Temple
  • Hotel check-in and overnight in Sigiriya
Day 2Sigiriya & Village Experience

Begin early at the UNESCO-listed rock fortress, then slow the pace with a traditional village experience on the water and a Sri Lankan lunch.

  • Early visit to Sigiriya Rock Fortress
  • Traditional village tour
  • Catamaran or canoe ride
  • Traditional Sri Lankan lunch
  • Free evening and overnight in Sigiriya
Day 3Polonnaruwa & Wildlife Safari

Explore the royal ruins and stone-carved Buddha figures of Polonnaruwa, followed by an afternoon jeep safari in elephant country.

  • Ancient City of Polonnaruwa
  • Gal Vihara and the ancient ruins
  • Lunch break
  • Minneriya or Kaudulla National Park jeep safari
  • Return to Sigiriya for the night
Day 4Pidurangala to the Airport

Climb Pidurangala for a memorable sunrise view, then return for breakfast and begin the private transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport.

  • Sunrise climb at Pidurangala
  • Breakfast and hotel check-out
  • Airport transfer and drop-off
