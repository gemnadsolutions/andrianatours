YOUR ROUTE THROUGH SRI LANKA

7-Day Sri Lanka Journey

A lively week across the island, from ancient Sigiriya and Ella’s green highlands to the surf coast and the quiet beaches of Tangalle.

DAY BY DAY

The journey, unfolded.

Each day can be adjusted around your pace, interests and arrival details.

Day 1Airport to Sigiriya

Meet your driver at the airport and travel into the Cultural Triangle, stopping at Dambulla before reaching Sigiriya.

  • Airport pickup
  • Dambulla Cave Temple
  • Sigiriya village or sunset experience
  • Overnight in Sigiriya
Day 2Sigiriya to Ella

Climb Sigiriya early, then settle into a scenic drive through changing landscapes toward Ella.

  • Early Sigiriya Rock Fortress visit
  • Drive to Ella
  • Mountain viewpoints
  • Overnight in Ella
Day 3Ella

Spend a full day around Ella’s best-known viewpoints, bridge, waterfall and tea country.

  • Nine Arch Bridge
  • Little Adam’s Peak
  • Ravana Falls
  • Tea plantation visit
  • Overnight in Ella
Day 4Ella to Arugam Bay

Leave the hills for the eastern coast and arrive in time to enjoy the relaxed surf atmosphere and sunset.

  • Scenic drive from Ella
  • Arugam Bay beach and surf area
  • Sunset by the coast
  • Overnight in Arugam Bay
Day 5Arugam Bay to Tangalle

Travel south through countryside and local villages, with beach stops before an evening in Tangalle.

  • Countryside drive
  • Beach stops and local villages
  • Tangalle sunset
  • Overnight in Tangalle
Day 6Tangalle

A slow beach day with the option to visit Hiriketiya or another nearby bay.

  • Tangalle beach time
  • Hiriketiya or nearby beach
  • Relaxed evening
  • Overnight in Tangalle
Day 7Tangalle to the Airport

Enjoy a final coastal morning before the expressway transfer, with an optional stop at Galle Fort when timing allows.

  • Beach morning
  • Optional Galle Fort visit
  • Expressway transfer to the airport
